Kind Words

What it sounds like when the weight actually lifts.

"I do not wait for things to fall apart before I fix them. I look ahead, flag what is coming, and quietly close the gaps you did not even know were open."

Before I ever set foot at Ecobank, I had already worked as an executive assistant, learning what it takes to keep someone else's day running without a single thing slipping. That experience shaped how I showed up at Ecobank, where I sat across from real customers every day and learned what it means to make someone feel heard and handled without being rushed. Both of those habits followed me into the freelance work I do now.

These days I work as a freelance Executive Virtual Assistant and Operations Professional, supporting founders and executives from behind the scenes. I have completed the ALX Virtual Assistant Program and hold a CRM Specialization from Coursera, and I bring both to every inbox, pipeline and client conversation I touch.

I am based in Lagos, and I built my whole setup around reliability. Constant power, backup internet, and a habit of showing up early rather than on time, because the clients I work with should never have to think twice about whether I will be online when they need me.
